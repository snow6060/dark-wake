associated hosted services (together, "Dark Wake").

## 1. Summary

- Dark Wake does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your
  name, email address, or other profile information.
- The app sends requests to our hosted API to display market prices, news,
  maritime-route data, and saved Strategy Desk analyses.
- Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request
  information, such as your IP address and request time, to deliver and secure
  the service.
- The Strategy Desk sends market and public-source context to the Meta Model API
  to generate its analysis. Dark Wake does not submit user-authored prompts because
  the app has no prompt-entry feature.
- Dark Wake does not include advertising, behavioral ad tracking, or a
  third-party analytics SDK.

## 2. Information we handle

### Information you provide

Dark Wake has no account registration, profile, contact form, or feature for
submitting personal information. We do not intentionally collect personal
information directly from you through the app.

If you contact us outside the app, we will handle the information in your
message to respond to you.

### Technical request information

When the app connects to the hosted API, the hosting provider and backend may
process technical information needed to handle and troubleshoot the request.
This can include an IP address, request date and time, requested API path and
method, and error or service diagnostic details. The app makes periodic requests
for current data while it is open; opening the Strategy Desk requests saved analysis
history.

The exact logs and retention periods depend on the hosting and infrastructure
configuration in effect. **[Publisher: confirm the deployed Render and other
provider log-retention settings and state the applicable period or link to the
provider's policy.]**

### Public market, news, and maritime data

The backend retrieves public or provider-supplied information, including Brent
market data, energy news, and aggregate chokepoint vessel-transit information.
It stores some of that information, together with derived summaries and
timestamps, to operate the app. These records are about markets, news, and
shipping activity; they are not collected from your device as personal
information.

### Strategy Desk analysis data

When a Strategy Desk analysis is generated, the backend sends Meta Model API
market context and related public-source information so it can produce the
analysis. This context can include prices and technical indicators for energy
commodities, public headlines, aggregate maritime telemetry, and a summary of
the previous saved analysis. The resulting analysis and transcript are saved
in the configured database so they can be shown to app users. Forecast
directions and outcomes may also be stored for aggregate agent-accuracy
calculations.

The Strategy Desk does not receive a user-entered prompt or account identity from the
app. Do not treat model output as private or confidential; it is saved as shared
application content and may be returned to all users of the service.

## 3. How information is used

We and our service providers use the information described above to:

- Deliver the app's prices, news, maritime telemetry, and Strategy Desk features.
- Retrieve and store public-source data and generated debate results.
- Keep the API available, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and maintain
  security.
- Respond to inquiries and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use
app request metadata to build advertising profiles.

## 4. Service providers and data sources

Dark Wake uses third-party services to host the backend, store application
records, retrieve public data, or generate analysis. Depending on the feature
and current deployment, these may include:

- **Render** — hosts the backend API and may process connection and service-log
  information.
- **Supabase** — stores configured application records, such as news, saved
  Strategy Desk analyses, and forecast-accuracy records.
- **Meta Model API** — processes the market and public-source context
  submitted by the backend to generate Strategy Desk analysis.
- **Yahoo Finance** — provides market data to the backend.
- **TankerMap**, **Straits.live**, and **IMF PortWatch** — provide public
  maritime or chokepoint information to the backend.
- **OilPrice**, **CNBC**, and the **U.S. Energy Information Administration
  (EIA)** — provide news feeds used by the backend.

The backend, rather than the desktop app, normally makes requests to these
upstream data providers. Those providers may process information about the
backend's requests under their own terms and privacy policies. The list may
change if the app's data sources or hosting configuration change.

We do not control third-party services. Their handling of information is
governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

## 5. Storage and retention

Application records are stored in the database services configured by the
publisher. The backend may also keep operational data in its configured local
database. No fixed deletion schedule is currently promised for saved public
data, news records, or debate history.

Technical request logs may be kept by the hosting or infrastructure provider
according to its configuration and policies. **[Publisher: confirm actual
retention periods for Render logs, Supabase records, and any other deployed
storage before publishing this policy.]**

Because Dark Wake does not create user accounts or associate saved analyses
with individual users, we may not be able to identify or delete a particular
user's records. You may contact us about a privacy request using the details
below.

## 6. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The Windows desktop app does not use browser cookies for advertising or
cross-site tracking. The current app does not include an advertising SDK or a
third-party analytics SDK. The hosted service and its infrastructure providers
may maintain technical logs for service operation and security, as described
above.

## 7. Security

The desktop app communicates with the configured hosted API over HTTPS.
Provider credentials used by the backend are configured server-side and are
not intended to be included in the desktop installer. We use reasonable
technical and organizational measures appropriate to operating the service.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop the app from making requests by closing it or uninstalling it.
Uninstalling the desktop app does not delete records held by the hosted
backend, database, or service-provider logs.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to,
correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on personal information, or to
object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details
below. We may need information sufficient to understand and respond to your
request; do not send passwords, API keys, or other secrets.

Since Dark Wake does not ask for an identity or maintain user accounts, we may
not be able to match a request to backend logs or identify records belonging to
a particular user. We will explain if that prevents us from fulfilling a
request.

## 10. Children's privacy

Dark Wake is a market-research application and is not directed to children.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the
app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact
us so we can review the request.
